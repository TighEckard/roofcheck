How to read and compare roofing estimates

Two quotes for the same roof can look very different. Here's how to compare them fairly.

Updated November 18, 2026 · By the Bedrock Roof team

Quick answer: A good roofing estimate is written and itemized. It names the shingle or panel brand and product line, says whether the old roof is torn off, lists underlayment, ice and water shield, flashing, drip edge and ventilation, explains how rotten decking is priced, and includes the permit, cleanup, warranty and payment schedule. Compare quotes on the same scope, not just the bottom line.

What should be on the estimate

  • Company details: legal name, address, phone, and license number where your state licenses roofers.
  • Roof size in squares (1 square = 100 sq ft) and the waste factor.
  • Tear-off: how many layers are removed, and disposal.
  • Decking: price per sheet for replacing rotten wood.
  • Underlayment type, and ice and water shield locations.
  • Flashing: new or reused at chimneys, walls and skylights.
  • Ventilation: ridge vents, intake vents, any changes.
  • Roof covering: brand, product line and color.
  • Permit, cleanup, warranty and payment schedule.

How to compare quotes fairly

  1. Put the quotes side by side and check each line above.
  2. Ask the lower bidder what's missing, and the higher bidder what's extra.
  3. Check that the same product line is quoted. "Architectural shingles" can mean very different products.
  4. Compare workmanship warranty length, not just the manufacturer's.

Red flags

  • A one-line quote ("new roof: $X") with no detail.
  • A large cash deposit before materials arrive.
  • Pressure to sign today or "this price expires tonight."
  • An offer to cover or "waive" your insurance deductible, which is illegal in some states.

Frequently asked questions

How many roofing quotes should I get?
For a full replacement, two or three written quotes is a common rule of thumb. For a small repair, one trusted roofer may be enough.
Why are roofing quotes so different?
Different products, scope (tear-off, decking, flashing, ventilation), labor costs, overhead and warranty length. Line up the scope first.
What is a roofing square?
A square is 100 square feet of roof surface. A roof is measured in squares, plus extra for waste on cuts, hips and valleys.
How much deposit is normal for a roof?
It varies, and some states cap deposits on home improvement work. Be cautious about paying a large share before materials are delivered.
