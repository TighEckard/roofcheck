Get a free roof inspection from a local roofer

Tell us about your roof and we'll connect you with an independent local roofer for a free inspection and a written quote.

Updated November 18, 2026 · By the Bedrock Roof team

How it works: fill in the short form on this page (about 30 seconds). We match your request with an independent roofer who serves your ZIP code. They call or text you to set up a time, inspect the roof, and give you their findings and a written quote if work is needed. The inspection is free and you don't have to hire anyone.

Good times to get a free inspection

  • After hail, high wind or a hurricane, even if you don't see damage from the ground.
  • When you find a leak, a ceiling stain or shingles in the yard.
  • When the roof is 15 years old or more.
  • Before you buy or sell a home.
  • If your insurer has asked about your roof's age or condition.

What the roofer checks

Shingles or panels, flashing around chimneys and walls, pipe boots and vents, gutters, signs of hail or wind damage, and the attic if it's accessible. Ask for photos of anything they find. See what happens during a roof inspection.

Why use Bedrock Roof

  • Free for you. You never pay us. Roofers may pay us for introductions.
  • One local roofer, not a flood of calls. We try to match each request with one roofer who serves your area.
  • No pressure. You can get more quotes and take your time. Our roofer checklist helps you compare.

Bedrock Roof is a referral service, not a roofing contractor. Roofers are independent businesses. Check any contractor's license and insurance before you sign.

Frequently asked questions

Is the roof inspection really free?
Yes. The roofer we connect you with offers a free inspection and quote, and you never pay Bedrock Roof. You only pay if you choose to hire a roofer for work.
How soon can someone come out?
It depends on the roofer's schedule and the weather, especially after big storms. Most roofers reach out within a business day to set a time.
Will the roofer help with my insurance claim?
Many roofers document storm damage with photos and an estimate you can give your insurer. Your insurance company decides what's covered.
Do you inspect commercial roofs?
Yes. Choose Commercial or Apts / HOA on the form and we'll look for a commercial roofer. See our commercial inspection page.
