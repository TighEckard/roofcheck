Tile roofs: clay vs. concrete, lifespan and repairs

Tile roofs are common in Florida, the Southwest and California. Here's what owners and buyers should know.

Updated November 18, 2026 · By the Bedrock Roof team

Quick answer: Clay and concrete tiles can last 50 years or more, and they resist fire and heat well. They cost more than shingles and are much heavier, so the framing must be able to carry them. On many tile roofs, the underlayment beneath the tiles wears out long before the tiles do, so a "tile roof replacement" sometimes means lifting the tiles, replacing the underlayment and putting the tiles back.

Clay vs. concrete

Clay tileConcrete tile
LookClassic terracotta; color goes through the tileMany shapes and colors; some colors fade over time
WeightHeavyUsually heavier than clay
CostGenerally higherGenerally lower than clay
LifespanOften 50+ yearsOften 40–50+ years

Strengths and weak spots

  • Strengths: long life, Class A fire ratings, good in heat, and a look many buyers want.
  • Weak spots: tiles can crack if walked on or hit by large hail, and the underlayment underneath ages.

Common repairs

  • Replacing cracked or slipped tiles. Keep spare tiles from the original job if you can, since matching older tiles can be hard.
  • Re-setting ridge and hip tiles.
  • Fixing flashing at valleys and walls. See roof flashing.
  • Lift-and-relay: removing tiles, installing new underlayment and reinstalling the tiles.

Hiring for tile work

Not every roofer does tile. Ask how many tile roofs the crew has done, how they walk on the roof without cracking tiles, and whether the quote includes new underlayment and flashing. In hurricane areas, ask how the tiles will be fastened to meet local wind rules.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a tile roof last?
The tiles often last 50 years or more. The underlayment beneath them usually needs replacing sooner, which can be done by lifting and relaying the tiles.
Can you walk on a tile roof?
Carefully, and ideally only by an experienced roofer. Tiles can crack under weight if stepped on in the wrong place.
Is tile more expensive than shingles?
Yes, tile generally costs more to buy and install, but it can last much longer.
Can my house support a tile roof?
Tile is heavy. Switching from shingles to tile may need an engineer to confirm the framing can carry it.
