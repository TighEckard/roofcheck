About Bedrock Roof

We help homeowners take the first step on a roof project: getting a qualified local roofer to come take a look.

Bedrock Roof is a free referral service for homeowners across the United States. You tell us what's going on with your roof, and we connect you with an independent roofing contractor serving your area for a free inspection and quote.

What we believe

Hiring a roofer shouldn't mean dozens of sales calls or pressure to sign on the spot. We keep the process simple, we're clear about how we're paid, and we publish plain-English guides so you can make your own decision.

How we're paid

Roofing contractors may pay us for introductions to homeowners who asked to be contacted. Homeowners never pay us.

What we don't do

We're not a roofing contractor. We don't perform, guarantee or warranty roofing work, and we don't decide insurance claims. Every roofer is an independent business, so please verify the license and insurance of any contractor before hiring.
