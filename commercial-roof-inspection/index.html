What a commercial inspection usually covers

  • Membrane condition, seams and punctures on TPO, EPDM, PVC, modified bitumen or built-up roofs.
  • Ponding water, drains, scuppers and gutters.
  • Flashing at parapet walls, curbs, HVAC units and other penetrations.
  • Roof edges and metal coping.
  • Signs of wet insulation; ask whether core samples or an infrared scan are recommended.

When to request one

  • Twice a year as routine maintenance, ideally spring and fall.
  • After hail, high wind or heavy snow.
  • When tenants report leaks or stains.
  • Before buying a building, renewing insurance or planning a reserve study.

What to ask for afterward

Photos, a written condition report, and options: repair, restoration coating, or replacement, each with scope and warranty. Our guides on commercial roof cost drivers, TPO vs EPDM vs PVC and commercial warranties help you compare.

Is a commercial roof inspection free?
The commercial roofer we connect you with offers a free inspection and quote. Specialized testing, like an infrared moisture survey, may cost extra; ask first.
How often should a commercial roof be inspected?
Most roofing groups recommend twice a year and after major storms.
Can you help HOAs and apartment owners?
