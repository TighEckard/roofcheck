9 signs you might need a new roof

Roofs rarely fail all at once. These are the signs roofers look for when deciding whether it's time for a new one.

Updated November 18, 2026 · By the Bedrock Roof team

  1. Age. If your roof is approaching the typical lifespan for its material, it's worth an inspection. See how long roofs last.
  2. Curling or cupping shingles. Edges that turn up or centers that sink are signs of wear or poor ventilation.
  3. Cracked or missing shingles, especially across several areas of the roof.
  4. Granules in the gutters. Shingles shed granules as they age, and heavy loss means less protection.
  5. Daylight in the attic or water stains on rafters and decking.
  6. Ceiling stains or leaks inside the home.
  7. A sagging roof line, which can mean water damage or structural problems and should be checked soon.
  8. Damaged flashing around chimneys, vents and skylights.
  9. Rising energy bills together with an old roof can point to ventilation or insulation problems.

One sign alone doesn't always mean you need a whole new roof. A good roofer will explain what they see and whether a repair is enough.

Frequently asked questions

Is a roof inspection worth it if I don't see a leak?
Often yes, especially after a big storm or if the roof is getting older. Small problems are cheaper to fix before they become leaks.
Are black streaks on my roof a problem?
They're usually algae. It's mostly cosmetic, though it can be cleaned, and algae-resistant shingles prevent it.
