How much does roof repair cost?

Typical national prices for common roof repairs, and how to tell a fair repair quote from a push to replace.

Updated November 18, 2026 · By the Bedrock Roof team

Quick answer: Most roof repairs in the U.S. cost between about $393 and $1,934, with an average of about $1,155. Small fixes like a vent boot or a patched hole can be a few hundred dollars, while hail damage or sagging can run into the thousands. A full replacement typically costs about $5,900 to $12,900 by comparison. Source: Angi survey of more than 30,000 customers (2025 data).

Other costs to expect

  • Inspection: about $75 to $600 when it isn't free.
  • Emergency or after-hours call: often an extra $100 to $300 or more.
  • Labor: roughly $45 to $75 per hour.

Repair or replace?

Repair usually makes sense when the damage is limited to one area and the roof has years of life left. Replacement starts to make sense when repairs are adding up, damage is spread across the roof, or the roof is near the end of its life. If one repair would cost a large share of a new roof, get a replacement quote too. See repair or replace and use our roof cost calculator to compare.

How to get a fair repair price

  1. Ask for photos of the damage and a written scope before work starts.
  2. Ask whether the price includes matching shingles and flashing, not just sealant.
  3. If the damage came from a storm, ask the roofer to document it before you call your insurer. See filing a roof claim.
  4. Be careful with anyone who finds damage you can't see in the photos. See roofing scams.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to fix a roof leak?
Angi reports most roof leak repairs cost about $360 to $1,550, depending on the cause, how easy it is to reach and how much water damage there is underneath.
Is it worth repairing an old roof?
If the damage is small and the rest of the roof is sound, a repair can buy years. If the roof is near the end of its expected life or problems keep coming back, putting the money toward a replacement is often better value.
Does insurance pay for roof repairs?
It may if the damage came from a sudden covered event like hail, wind or a fallen tree, minus your deductible. Wear and tear and slow leaks from age usually aren't covered.
Can I get a free roof repair estimate?
Many roofers inspect and quote repairs for free. You can request a free inspection from a local roofer on this page.
