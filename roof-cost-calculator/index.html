How the calculator works

  1. Roof area. We divide your living space by the number of stories to get the footprint, add 10% for overhangs, then multiply by a standard pitch factor (1.05 for a low 4/12 roof up to 1.41 for a 12/12 roof). If you enter your roof area, we use that instead.
  2. Base price. Roof area times $4 to $11 per square foot, the national installed range for a typical asphalt roof reported by NerdWallet from Angi data.
  3. Material. For other materials we add the difference between that material's cost per square foot and asphalt's ($1 to $1.20), using the same source: synthetic $5–$12, wood $6–$15, metal $6–$16, clay or concrete tile $10–$20, slate $10–$30 per square foot.
  4. Steep roofs. A steeply sloped roof can add about $1,000 to $3,000, per the same source.

Results are rounded to the nearest $100. They don't include extended warranties (about $500 to $5,000), permit fees (often up to $500) or rotted decking, which a roofer can only price after an inspection.

Why your real quote may differ

  • Local labor. Labor is often more than half the bill and runs higher in expensive metros and right after big storms.
  • Tear-off. Removing one or more old layers adds labor and disposal.
  • Decking. Soft or rotted boards are replaced per sheet once the old roof is off. Ask for that price in writing.
  • Roof shape. Valleys, dormers, chimneys and skylights add flashing work.
  • Insurance. If hail or wind damaged the roof, your policy may cover part of it. See does insurance cover roof replacement?

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is a roof cost calculator?
It gives a ballpark range based on national data. Your actual price depends on local labor, your roof's shape, how many layers need to come off and whether the decking needs repair. Only a written quote after an inspection is exact.
How do I measure my roof area?
Roofers measure it for you during an inspection, often from satellite imagery. As a rough guide, roof area is your home's footprint plus overhangs, multiplied by a factor for the pitch: about 1.05 for a low roof and up to about 1.41 for a very steep one.
What is a roofing square?
A square is 100 square feet of roof. A 2,000 sq ft roof is 20 squares. Quotes and material orders are usually priced by the square.
Why is my quote higher than the calculator?
Common reasons are higher local labor rates, a steep or complex roof, extra layers to tear off, decking repairs, upgraded underlayment or an extended warranty. Ask the roofer to itemize the quote, or get a free second opinion.
Does homeowners insurance pay for a new roof?
It may, if the roof was damaged by a covered event like hail or wind. Wear and tear from age usually isn't covered, and the final decision is your insurer's.
