Should you replace your roof before going solar?

Solar panels can last 25 years or more. If your roof won't, plan ahead.

Updated November 18, 2026 · By the Bedrock Roof team

Quick answer: If your roof is more than about halfway through its expected life, or a roofer finds problems, it's usually smarter to replace it before installing solar. Solar panels commonly last 25 years or more, and removing and reinstalling them later for a roof replacement adds cost and hassle. Get a roof inspection first, and coordinate the roofer and solar installer so penetrations and flashing are done right.

Why it matters

  • Panels cover much of the roof, making later repairs harder.
  • Detach-and-reset of panels for a reroof is a separate, often significant expense.
  • Some solar warranties cover roof penetrations only if the roof was in good shape.

How to decide

  1. Find your roof's age and material. See how long roofs last.
  2. Get a roof inspection and a written opinion on remaining life.
  3. If replacement is likely within the next several years, do it first.

Coordinating the two jobs

  • Ask whether the solar installer or roofer will flash the mounts, and who warranties leaks.
  • Consider durable materials under the array, like architectural shingles or metal.
  • Keep records of both warranties together.

Frequently asked questions

How old is too old for a roof before solar?
There's no single cutoff, but many installers want a roof with at least as much remaining life as the panel warranty or plan for replacement first. An inspection gives a better answer than age alone.
Does installing solar damage the roof?
Properly installed mounts are flashed and sealed. Leaks usually trace back to poor installation, so choose an installer with a workmanship warranty.
Can I bundle roof replacement into solar financing?
Some solar companies and lenders allow it. Compare the total cost and terms with separate financing.
Is metal roofing good for solar?
Yes. Standing seam metal allows clamp-on mounts without penetrations and outlasts most panels.
