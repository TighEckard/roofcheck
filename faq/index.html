FAQ

Questions homeowners ask

Straight answers about how Bedrock Roof works.

All questions

Is Bedrock Roof free for homeowners?
Yes. You never pay Bedrock Roof anything. Roofing contractors may pay us for introductions, which is how we keep the service free for you.
Is Bedrock Roof a roofing company?
No. We are a referral service. We don't install or repair roofs. We connect you with an independent local roofing contractor, and any work is between you and that contractor.
How many roofers will contact me?
We try to match each request with one local roofer instead of sending your number to a long list of companies. You can always get more quotes on your own, and we recommend at least two or three for a full replacement.
Do I have to hire the roofer?
No. The inspection and quote are free and there's no obligation to hire anyone.
Do you help with commercial roofs?
Yes. Owners and managers of commercial buildings, apartments, condos and HOAs can request a free inspection and quote from an independent commercial roofer. See our commercial roofing guide.
Which areas do you serve?
Homeowners anywhere in the United States can send a request. If we can't find a roofer serving your ZIP code, we'll tell you.
Can a roofer help with an insurance claim?
Many roofers will document storm damage with photos and a written estimate you can give your insurer. Whether a claim is covered is up to your insurance company, and nobody can promise approval.
What happens after I submit the form?
We pass your request to an independent roofer serving your ZIP code, who will call or text you to set up a free inspection.
Is the inspection really free?
Roofers in our referrals offer free inspections and quotes for homeowners who request them. If a roofer ever asks to charge you for an inspection, you're free to say no.
Do you check roofers' licenses and insurance?
Roofers are independent businesses and we don't guarantee their work. Always verify the license with your state or local licensing board and ask for current insurance certificates before you sign. Our checklist shows you how.
Will you sell my information?
We share your request only with the roofer we match you with so they can contact you about your roof. We don't sell your information to marketing lists. See our privacy policy.
How do I stop calls or texts?
Reply STOP to any text, tell the caller you're no longer interested, or email hello@bedrockroof.com.

Ready for a quote?

Tell us about your roof and a local roofer will reach out. Free, no obligation.
