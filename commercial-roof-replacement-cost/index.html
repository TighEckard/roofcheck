What drives commercial roof replacement cost?

Commercial roof prices vary widely, so it helps to know what's actually driving the number in each bid.

Updated November 18, 2026 · By the Bedrock Roof team

Quick answer: Commercial roofs are usually priced per square foot or per "square" (100 square feet), and the biggest drivers are the roof system, whether the old roof must be torn off, how much insulation is added, the building's height and access, and the warranty you want. Bids for the same building can differ a lot, so compare scope line by line rather than just the total. We don't publish a single average price, because it would be misleading across U.S. regions and building types.

The main cost drivers

  • Roof system: TPO, EPDM, PVC, modified bitumen, built-up, metal or a coating. See TPO vs EPDM vs PVC.
  • Tear-off or recover: removing old layers costs more but finds wet insulation and deck damage. Codes limit how many layers you can have.
  • Insulation: energy codes often require added insulation on a replacement, and tapered insulation fixes ponding water.
  • Deck repairs: rusted metal deck or rotted wood is often priced per unit once exposed. Ask for those unit prices in the bid.
  • Penetrations and details: HVAC curbs, drains, skylights, parapet walls and edge metal add labor.
  • Access and height: cranes, hoists, street permits and working around tenants or business hours.
  • Warranty level: a manufacturer's full system warranty usually requires specific materials and inspections, which can raise cost.

How to compare bids

  1. Give every bidder the same scope, ideally in writing.
  2. Ask for core samples or an infrared or moisture survey so everyone prices the same conditions.
  3. Get unit prices for deck and insulation replacement.
  4. Confirm who pulls the permit and who handles HVAC disconnects.
  5. Check the contractor's license and insurance with your state. See choosing a roofer.

Repair, coat or replace?

If the insulation is mostly dry and the membrane is sound, a repair or a restoration coating may extend the roof's life for less. Widespread wet insulation or repeated leaks usually mean replacement. Planned maintenance helps either way; see the maintenance checklist.

Frequently asked questions

How is commercial roofing priced?
Usually per square foot or per square (100 square feet), plus line items for tear-off, insulation, deck repairs, flashing and edge metal.
Why are commercial roof bids so different?
Bidders often assume different scopes, such as recover vs tear-off, insulation thickness or warranty level. Give everyone the same written scope.
Is a roof coating cheaper than replacement?
Usually, when the existing roof is sound and mostly dry. A coating won't fix wet insulation or a failing deck.
Can roof replacement be deducted for a business?
Tax treatment of building improvements varies. Ask your accountant how a roof replacement is handled for your property.
