Commercial or flat roof leak: what to do first

Leaks on flat roofs are hard to trace because water travels. Here's how to respond quickly and in the right order.

Updated November 18, 2026 · By the Bedrock Roof team

Quick answer: Protect people and equipment first, then document the leak with photos, times and weather. Check for clogged drains and ponding water, which cause many flat-roof leaks. If the roof is under a manufacturer or contractor warranty, call the warranty holder before anyone else works on it, so the warranty isn't voided. Water travels under membranes, so the leak inside is often not directly under the hole.

First hour

  1. Move people and equipment, and cut power to anything wet if it's safe to do so.
  2. Contain water with buckets, tarps and plastic sheeting.
  3. Photograph the ceiling, floor, and any staining; note the time and the weather.
  4. If it's safe, have maintenance staff check that roof drains and scuppers aren't clogged.

Finding the source

  • Common culprits: clogged drains, seams, flashing at walls and curbs, HVAC units, pitch pans and roof edges.
  • Ponding water that sits more than a couple of days after rain stresses membranes and seams.
  • Infrared or electronic leak detection can locate wet insulation and breaches without tearing up the roof.

Warranty and insurance

  • Check whether the roof has a manufacturer warranty and a separate contractor workmanship warranty. Many require that only authorized contractors make repairs.
  • Storm-related leaks may be covered by property insurance. Document everything and notify your carrier promptly. See roof insurance claims.

Preventing the next one

Twice-yearly inspections and after-storm checks catch most problems early. Use our commercial roof maintenance checklist.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my flat roof leak only sometimes?
Leaks that appear with wind-driven rain or heavy storms often come from flashing, edges or HVAC curbs. Leaks after any rain often point to drains, seams or punctures.
Is the leak above where the water drips?
Not always. Water can travel across the deck or through insulation before it shows up inside.
Should I call the original installer?
If the roof is under warranty, yes. Repairs by others can void some warranties.
How fast should a commercial leak be fixed?
As soon as possible. Wet insulation loses value and can lead to deck rust or rot and mold.
