How it works

How Bedrock Roof works

A free, simple way to get a roofer out to look at your roof. Here's exactly what happens, and how we're paid.

1. You send a request

Choose what you need (new roof, repair, storm damage or inspection) and enter your name, mobile number and ZIP code. A short note helps the roofer come prepared.

2. We match you with a local roofer

We pass your request to an independent roofing contractor that serves your ZIP code. We aim to send each request to one roofer so your phone doesn't ring off the hook. If we can't find anyone in your area, we'll let you know.

3. The roofer contacts you

The roofer calls or texts to set up a free inspection. They'll look at the roof, answer your questions and give you a written quote.

4. You decide

There's no obligation. Compare the quote with others, check the contractor's license and insurance (here's how) and hire only if it's right for you. Any contract is between you and the roofer.

How we make money

Bedrock Roof is free for homeowners. Roofing contractors may pay us a fee for introductions to homeowners who asked to be contacted. That fee never gets added to your bill by us, and it doesn't change your right to say no.

What we are not

We are not a roofing contractor and we don't perform, inspect, guarantee or warranty roofing work. We don't handle insurance claims and can't promise any claim will be approved. Roofers are independent businesses, so always verify a roofer's license and insurance yourself before signing.
