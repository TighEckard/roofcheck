Flat roof types compared: TPO, EPDM, PVC and more

Flat and low-slope roofs need different materials than shingled roofs. Here's how the main systems compare.

Updated November 18, 2026 · By the Bedrock Roof team

Quick answer: The most common flat-roof systems are TPO (white, reflective, heat-welded seams), EPDM (durable rubber, popular in cold climates), PVC (heat-welded and grease-resistant, common on restaurants), modified bitumen and built-up roofing. Most last about 15 to 30 years. Installation quality, drainage and seam details matter as much as the material.

Side-by-side comparison

SystemWhat it isOften used forTypical lifespan
TPOWhite, reflective; heat-welded seamsHot climates; most new low-slope roofs15 to 30 years
EPDMBlack (or white) rubber; glued or taped seamsCold climates; long track record20 to 30+ years
PVCHeat-welded; resists grease and chemicalsRestaurants, chemical exposure20 to 30 years
Modified bitumenAsphalt sheets in layersSmall flat roofs, foot traffic15 to 25 years
Built-up roof (BUR)Layers of bitumen and felt, often with gravelOlder commercial buildings20 to 30 years
Coating over existing roofSilicone, acrylic or polyurethaneExtending a sound existing roof10 to 20 years, can be renewed

Lifespans are typical ranges and depend on membrane thickness, installation, climate, drainage and maintenance.

What matters besides the membrane

  • Drainage. Tapered insulation, drains and scuppers keep water from ponding.
  • Insulation. Replacing a roof is often the time to bring insulation up to the current energy code, which may be required.
  • Attachment. Membranes can be mechanically fastened, fully adhered or ballasted. Wind zone and building type drive the choice.
  • Edges and flashing. Most flat-roof leaks start at seams, edges, walls and penetrations, not in the middle of the membrane.
  • Warranty. Manufacturer warranties vary from material-only to full system coverage and often require a certified installer and regular maintenance.

Homes with flat roofs

Rowhouses, mid-century modern homes and additions often have flat or low-slope sections. The same materials apply, and shingles should never be used on a roof that's too flat for them.

Frequently asked questions

Which is better, TPO or EPDM?
Neither is best everywhere. TPO is white and reflective with heat-welded seams, which suits hot climates and cooling-heavy buildings. EPDM is a durable rubber membrane with a long track record, popular in colder climates. Installation quality and the details at seams and edges matter as much as the membrane.
How long does a flat roof last?
Single-ply and modified bitumen roofs commonly last about 15 to 30 years, depending on the product, thickness, installation, climate and maintenance.
Is ponding water on a flat roof a problem?
Water that stands for more than about 48 hours after rain is generally a sign of a drainage or structural issue. It speeds up wear, can void some warranties and adds weight, so it's worth having a roofer look at it.
