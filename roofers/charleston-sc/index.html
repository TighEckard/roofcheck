Roofing in Charleston: what to know

Charleston is a low-lying coastal city exposed to hurricanes, tropical storms and salt air, and Hurricane Hugo in 1989 caused severe damage across the area. Many historic downtown homes have metal or slate roofs that need specialized repair.

What wears out roofs around Charleston

  • Hurricane and tropical-storm winds that lift shingles and drive rain under flashing.
  • Salt air near the water that corrodes metal flashing, fasteners and vents.

Popular roof choices around Charleston

Around Charleston, roofers often recommend high-wind-rated shingles, tile or standing-seam metal with a sealed roof deck, and corrosion-resistant flashing and fasteners.

Local tip: After a named storm, report damage to your insurer quickly and be careful with out-of-town crews going door to door. Some insurers here ask about a roof's age before renewing a policy, so keep your installation paperwork.

Checking a roofer's license in South Carolina

South Carolina licenses residential specialty contractors, including roofers, through the state Department of Labor, Licensing and Regulation. Verify the license on the LLR website.

Before you hire a roofer in Charleston

  1. Verify the license or registration as described above, and make sure the name matches the contract.
  2. Ask for current certificates of liability and workers' compensation insurance.
  3. Get a written, itemized quote and compare at least two.
  4. Make sure the roofer pulls the permit with the local building department.
  5. Get the material and workmanship warranties in writing.
