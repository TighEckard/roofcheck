Best roofing materials for Chandler

Around Chandler, roofers often recommend tile, light-colored or reflective 'cool roof' materials, and coatings on flat sections.

Best time to replace a roof in Chandler

Summer heat makes roof work slow and hard on materials, so many homeowners here schedule replacements for fall, winter or spring.

Local tip: Ask whether a tile roof needs a full replacement or an underlayment 'lift and relay', which reuses good tiles.

Chandler roofing questions

How much does a new roof cost in Chandler?
Nationally, Angi data reported by NerdWallet puts the average near $9,500, with most jobs between about $5,800 and $46,000. Your price in Chandler depends on size, pitch, material and local labor, so get a written quote.
Will insurance pay for roof damage in Chandler?
Most homeowners policies cover sudden damage, such as a tree falling on the roof or a storm tearing off shingles, but not wear and tear or an old roof failing.
Do I need a permit to replace my roof in Chandler?
Usually, yes. Your roofer should pull it with the Chandler building department or Maricopa County and show it to you before work starts.
