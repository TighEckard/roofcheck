What wears out roofs around Bayonne

  • Nor'easters and the occasional tropical storm bringing wind-driven rain.
  • Salt air near the water that corrodes metal flashing, fasteners and vents.
  • Snow loads and ice dams that push meltwater under shingles at the eaves.

Best roofing materials for Bayonne

Around Bayonne, roofers often recommend well-sealed flashing and wind-rated architectural shingles, corrosion-resistant flashing and fasteners, and an ice-and-water barrier along the eaves plus good attic insulation and ventilation.

Best time to replace a roof in Bayonne

Most roofers here work from spring through fall, because shingles seal best above about 40°F. An autumn check of flashing, gutters and attic ventilation helps prevent winter ice dams.
