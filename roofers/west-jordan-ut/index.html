Best time to replace a roof in West Jordan

Summer thunderstorms are the main hazard, so spring is a good time for an inspection. In damp, warm weather, watch for algae streaks and moss on the shady side of the roof.

Local tip: Black streaks are usually algae, not damage, but curling, missing or granule-shedding shingles are worth an inspection.

West Jordan roofing questions

How much does a new roof cost in West Jordan?
Nationally, Angi data reported by NerdWallet puts the average near $9,500, with most jobs between about $5,800 and $46,000. Your price in West Jordan depends on size, pitch, material and local labor, so get a written quote.
Will insurance pay for roof damage in West Jordan?
Most homeowners policies cover sudden damage, such as a tree falling on the roof or a storm tearing off shingles, but not wear and tear or an old roof failing.
Do I need a permit to replace my roof in West Jordan?
Usually, yes. Your roofer should pull it with the West Jordan building department or Salt Lake County and show it to you before work starts.
