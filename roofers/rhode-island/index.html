Common roof problems in Rhode Island

  • Nor'easters and the occasional tropical storm bringing wind-driven rain.
  • Salt air near the water that corrodes metal flashing, fasteners and vents.
  • Snow loads and ice dams that push meltwater under shingles at the eaves.

Asphalt shingles with an ice-and-water barrier along the eaves are the norm, and metal roofs shed snow well. Good attic insulation and ventilation matter as much as the shingles.

Tip: Many roofers here book up in late summer and fall, so get quotes early if you want the work done before winter.

Checking a roofer's license in Rhode Island

Rhode Island requires contractors, including roofers, to register with the Contractors' Registration and Licensing Board. Verify the registration online.

Ask every contractor for their license or registration number and check it before you sign. Our roofer checklist covers the rest.
