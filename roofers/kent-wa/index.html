Roof inspection in Kent

A good inspection covers the shingles or membrane, flashing, vents, gutters and the attic. Around Kent, roofers look closely at corroded flashing, fasteners and vents from salt air, plus moss, clogged gutters and soft spots from long wet seasons. If a winter windstorm leaves you with a leak or missing shingles, take photos first, then get the damaged area tarped to keep water out until a permanent repair. Asphalt shingle roofing in Kent

Asphalt shingles cover most U.S. homes because they're the most affordable option and any roofer can repair them. Architectural shingles usually carry higher wind ratings than basic 3-tab. Around Kent, ask about corrosion-resistant flashing and moss-resistant shingles or metal. See our roofing materials guide.

Flat and rubber (EPDM) roofs in Kent

Flat and low-slope roofs on homes (porches, additions, garages) and on commercial buildings are usually EPDM rubber, TPO, PVC or modified bitumen. With long wet seasons, keep drains and scuppers clear and ask about seams over the low spots. Read more on flat roof types.

How much does a new roof cost in Kent?
Nationally, Angi data reported by NerdWallet puts the average near $9,500, with most jobs between about $5,800 and $46,000. Your price in Kent depends on size, pitch, material and local labor, so get a written quote.
How much does roof repair cost in Kent?
Most roof repairs in the U.S. run about $393 to $1,934, about $1,155 on average (Angi survey). Small fixes like a vent boot or a few shingles cost less; hail damage or rotted decking costs more. A roofer serving Kent can give you a written price after a free inspection.
Will insurance pay for roof damage in Kent?
Most homeowners policies cover sudden damage, such as a tree falling on the roof or a storm tearing off shingles, but not wear and tear or an old roof failing.
