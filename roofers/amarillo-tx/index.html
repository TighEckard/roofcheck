Roofing in Amarillo: what to know

Amarillo is one of Texas's larger cities, with roughly 199,000 residents. It has a semi-arid climate, with dry air, strong sun and limited rain. It's well inland, so the main roof threats come from inland weather rather than the ocean.

What wears out roofs around Amarillo

  • Hailstorms that bruise and crack shingles, sometimes without an obvious leak.
  • Intense sun and heat that dry out and crack shingles and underlayment.

Popular roof choices around Amarillo

Around Amarillo, roofers often recommend impact-resistant (often Class 4) shingles or metal, which some insurers discount, and tile, light-colored or reflective 'cool roof' materials, and coatings on flat sections.

Local tip: Hail damage isn't always visible from the ground. If a storm hit your neighborhood, a roofer's inspection with photos can show what's there before you call your insurer.

Checking a roofer's license in Texas

Texas has no statewide license for roofers, so anyone can call themselves a roofer. That makes references, a local address, proof of insurance and a written contract especially important. Some Texas cities require roofers to register locally before pulling a permit.

Before you hire a roofer in Amarillo

  1. Verify the license or registration as described above, and make sure the name matches the contract.
  2. Ask for current certificates of liability and workers' compensation insurance.
  3. Get a written, itemized quote and compare at least two.
  4. Make sure the roofer pulls the permit with the local building department.
  5. Get the material and workmanship warranties in writing.
