Roofing in Glendale: what to know

Glendale is the sixth-largest city in Arizona, with roughly 240,000 residents, about 9 miles from Phoenix. It has a hot desert climate, with very hot summers and little rain. It's well inland, so the main roof threats come from inland weather rather than the ocean.

What wears out roofs around Glendale

  • Intense sun and heat that dry out and crack shingles and underlayment.
  • Wildfire embers in dry seasons that can ignite roofs and gutters.

Popular roof choices around Glendale

Around Glendale, roofers often recommend tile, light-colored or reflective 'cool roof' materials, and coatings on flat sections, and Class A fire-rated roofing and ember-resistant vents.

Local tip: Ask whether a tile roof needs a full replacement or an underlayment 'lift and relay', which reuses good tiles.

Checking a roofer's license in Arizona

Arizona contractors, including roofers, must be licensed by the Arizona Registrar of Contractors (ROC). You can search the ROC website by name or license number to see license status and complaint history.

Before you hire a roofer in Glendale

  1. Verify the license or registration as described above, and make sure the name matches the contract.
  2. Ask for current certificates of liability and workers' compensation insurance.
  3. Get a written, itemized quote and compare at least two.
  4. Make sure the roofer pulls the permit with the local building department.
  5. Get the material and workmanship warranties in writing.
