Roofing in Independence: what to know

Independence is the fifth-largest city in Missouri, with roughly 117,000 residents. It has a humid subtropical climate, with hot, humid summers and cooler winters. It's well inland, so the main roof threats come from inland weather rather than the ocean.

What wears out roofs around Independence

  • Hailstorms that bruise and crack shingles, sometimes without an obvious leak.
  • Summer thunderstorms and humidity that speed up shingle aging and algae streaks.

Popular roof choices around Independence

Around Independence, roofers often recommend impact-resistant (often Class 4) shingles or metal, which some insurers discount, and algae-resistant architectural shingles and good attic ventilation.

Local tip: Hail damage isn't always visible from the ground. If a storm hit your neighborhood, a roofer's inspection with photos can show what's there before you call your insurer.

Checking a roofer's license in Missouri

Missouri has no statewide roofing license. Some cities, including Kansas City and St. Louis area municipalities, require roofers to be licensed or registered locally, so ask which local licenses the roofer holds.

Before you hire a roofer in Independence

  1. Verify the license or registration as described above, and make sure the name matches the contract.
  2. Ask for current certificates of liability and workers' compensation insurance.
  3. Get a written, itemized quote and compare at least two.
  4. Make sure the roofer pulls the permit with the local building department.
  5. Get the material and workmanship warranties in writing.
