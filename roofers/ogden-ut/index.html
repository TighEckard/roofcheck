Best roofing materials for Ogden

Around Ogden, roofers often recommend an ice-and-water barrier along the eaves plus good attic insulation and ventilation, and Class A fire-rated roofing and ember-resistant vents.

Best time to replace a roof in Ogden

Most roofers here work from spring through fall, because shingles seal best above about 40°F. An autumn check of flashing, gutters and attic ventilation helps prevent winter ice dams.

A good inspection covers the shingles or membrane, flashing, vents, gutters and the attic. Asphalt shingle roofing in Ogden

Asphalt shingles cover most U.S. homes because they're the most affordable option and any roofer can repair them. Architectural shingles usually carry higher wind ratings than basic 3-tab. Flat and rubber (EPDM) roofs in Ogden
