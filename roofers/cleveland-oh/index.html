Roofing in Cleveland: what to know

Cleveland sits in the snow and freeze-thaw country, and that shapes what wears roofs out here and what homeowners tend to install. The most common causes of roof damage in this kind of climate are:

  • Ice dams that push water under shingles at the eaves.
  • Heavy snow loads on older or low-slope roofs.
  • Freeze-thaw cycles that crack flashing and sealants.

Popular roof types around Cleveland

Asphalt shingles with an ice-and-water barrier along the eaves are the norm, and metal roofs shed snow well. Good attic insulation and ventilation matter as much as the shingles.

Local tip: Many roofers here book up in late summer and fall, so get quotes early if you want the work done before winter.

Before you hire a roofer in Cleveland

  1. Verify the contractor's license with the Ohio or local licensing authority. Rules differ by state and city.
  2. Ask for current certificates of liability and workers' compensation insurance.
  3. Get a written, itemized quote and compare at least two.
  4. Make sure the roofer pulls the permit with the local building department.
  5. Get the material and workmanship warranties in writing.

Cleveland roofing questions

How do I find a good roofer in Cleveland?
Get two or three written quotes, check each contractor's license with the Ohio or local licensing authority, ask for proof of liability and workers' comp insurance, and read the warranty terms. Our roofer checklist walks you through it.
How much does a new roof cost in Cleveland?
It depends on the size, pitch and material of your roof and on local labor costs. Nationally, Angi data reported by NerdWallet puts the average around $9,500, with most jobs between about $5,800 and $46,000. A written quote after an inspection is the only way to know your price in Cleveland.

Do I need a permit to replace my roof in Cleveland?
Most U.S. cities require a building permit for a full roof replacement. Ask your roofer to pull the permit with the Cleveland or county building department and show it to you.
