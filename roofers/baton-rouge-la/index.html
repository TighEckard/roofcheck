What wears out roofs around Baton Rouge

  • Hurricane and tropical-storm winds that lift shingles and drive rain under flashing.
  • Severe spring storms with straight-line winds, hail and the occasional tornado.
  • Summer thunderstorms and humidity that speed up shingle aging and algae streaks.

Best roofing materials for Baton Rouge

Around Baton Rouge, roofers often recommend high-wind-rated shingles, tile or standing-seam metal with a sealed roof deck, wind-rated architectural shingles installed with the manufacturer's full nailing pattern, and algae-resistant architectural shingles and good attic ventilation.

Best time to replace a roof in Baton Rouge

Atlantic hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30, so the best time to fix weak spots is late winter or spring, before it starts. After a named storm, roofers get booked up fast.

Local tip: After a named storm, report damage to your insurer quickly and be careful with out-of-town crews going door to door. Some insurers here ask about a roof's age before renewing a policy, so keep your installation paperwork.

Baton Rouge roofing questions

How much does a new roof cost in Baton Rouge?
Nationally, Angi data reported by NerdWallet puts the average near $9,500, with most jobs between about $5,800 and $46,000. Your price in Baton Rouge depends on size, pitch, material and local labor, so get a written quote.
Will insurance pay for roof damage in Baton Rouge?
Most homeowners policies cover sudden storm damage, but many policies in hurricane-prone areas like Baton Rouge carry a separate hurricane or named-storm deductible, often a percentage of the home's insured value rather than a flat amount.
Do I need a permit to replace my roof in Baton Rouge?
Usually, yes. Your roofer should pull it with the Baton Rouge building department and show it to you before work starts.
