Common roof problems in West Virginia

  • Ice dams that push water under shingles at the eaves.
  • Heavy snow loads on older or low-slope roofs.
  • Freeze-thaw cycles that crack flashing and sealants.

Asphalt shingles with an ice-and-water barrier along the eaves are the norm, and metal roofs shed snow well. Good attic insulation and ventilation matter as much as the shingles.

Tip: Many roofers here book up in late summer and fall, so get quotes early if you want the work done before winter.

Checking a roofer's license in West Virginia

West Virginia requires contractors, including roofers, to be licensed by the Division of Labor. Check the license number before you hire.
