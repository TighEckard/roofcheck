Roofing in Arlington: what to know

Arlington sits between Dallas and Fort Worth in one of the most hail-prone areas in the country. Big spring hailstorms regularly lead to roof claims here, and impact-resistant shingles are a common upgrade.

What wears out roofs around Arlington

  • Hailstorms that bruise and crack shingles, sometimes without an obvious leak.
  • Summer thunderstorms and humidity that speed up shingle aging and algae streaks.

Best roofing materials for Arlington

Around Arlington, roofers often recommend impact-resistant (often Class 4) shingles or metal, which some insurers discount, and algae-resistant architectural shingles and good attic ventilation.

Best time to replace a roof in Arlington

Hail season peaks in spring and early summer. Have the roof checked after any storm with hail, since bruised shingles often don't leak until months later, and insurance claims usually have filing deadlines.

Local tip: Hail damage isn't always visible from the ground. If a storm hit your neighborhood, a roofer's inspection with photos can show what's there before you call your insurer.

Checking a roofer's license in Texas

Texas has no statewide license for roofers, so anyone can call themselves a roofer. That makes references, a local address, proof of insurance and a written contract especially important. Some Texas cities require roofers to register locally before pulling a permit. Our roofer checklist covers the rest.

Arlington roofing questions

How much does a new roof cost in Arlington?
Nationally, Angi data reported by NerdWallet puts the average near $9,500, with most jobs between about $5,800 and $46,000. Your price in Arlington depends on size, pitch, material and local labor, so get a written quote.
Will insurance pay for roof damage in Arlington?
Most homeowners policies cover sudden hail and wind damage, but in storm-prone areas like Arlington many policies carry a separate wind and hail deductible, and some pay only depreciated value on older roofs.
Do I need a permit to replace my roof in Arlington?
Usually, yes. Your roofer should pull it with the Arlington building department or Tarrant County and show it to you before work starts.
