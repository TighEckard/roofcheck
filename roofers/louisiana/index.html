Common roof problems in Louisiana

  • Severe spring storms with straight-line winds, hail and the occasional tornado.
  • Hurricane and tropical-storm winds that lift shingles and drive rain under flashing.
  • Summer thunderstorms and humidity that speed up shingle aging and algae streaks.
  • Salt air near the water that corrodes metal flashing, fasteners and vents.

Many homeowners here choose high-wind-rated architectural shingles, concrete or clay tile, or standing-seam metal. Ask roofers about wind ratings, secondary water barriers and how the roof deck is fastened.

Tip: After a named storm, report damage to your insurer quickly and be careful with out-of-town crews going door to door. Some insurers here ask about a roof's age before renewing a policy, so keep your installation paperwork.

Checking a roofer's license in Louisiana

Louisiana licenses contractors, including residential roofers on larger jobs, through the Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors. The board's website lets you search by name or license number.

Ask every contractor for their license or registration number and check it before you sign. Our roofer checklist covers the rest.
