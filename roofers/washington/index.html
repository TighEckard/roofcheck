Common roof problems in Washington

  • Long wet seasons, moss and algae that hold moisture against the roof.
  • Salt air near the water that corrodes metal flashing, fasteners and vents.

Architectural asphalt shingles and metal are common. Moss-resistant shingles, good ventilation and well-sealed flashing around chimneys and skylights matter here.

Tip: Dry summer months are the busiest season for roofers, so book inspections early.

Checking a roofer's license in Washington

Washington requires contractors, including roofers, to register with the Department of Labor & Industries (L&I), which also shows bond and insurance status. Look the roofer up on the L&I website.

Ask every contractor for their license or registration number and check it before you sign. Our roofer checklist covers the rest.
