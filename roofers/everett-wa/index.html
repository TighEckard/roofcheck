Roofing in Everett: what to know

Everett is the seventh-largest city in Washington, with roughly 108,000 residents, about 26 miles from Seattle. It has a dry-summer climate, with warm, dry summers and cool, wet winters. It sits right by the Pacific Ocean, so salt air and coastal storms are part of the picture for roofs here.

What wears out roofs around Everett

  • Salt air near the water that corrodes metal flashing, fasteners and vents.
  • Wildfire embers in dry seasons that can ignite roofs and gutters.
  • Long wet seasons, moss and algae that hold moisture against the roof.

Popular roof choices around Everett

Around Everett, roofers often recommend corrosion-resistant flashing and fasteners, Class A fire-rated roofing and ember-resistant vents, and moss-resistant shingles or metal, with careful flashing around chimneys and skylights.

Local tip: Ask roofers about local fire-rating requirements and whether your area needs a permit for a reroof (most do).

Checking a roofer's license in Washington

Washington requires contractors, including roofers, to register with the Department of Labor & Industries (L&I), which also shows bond and insurance status. Look the roofer up on the L&I website.

Before you hire a roofer in Everett

  1. Verify the license or registration as described above, and make sure the name matches the contract.
  2. Ask for current certificates of liability and workers' compensation insurance.
  3. Get a written, itemized quote and compare at least two.
  4. Make sure the roofer pulls the permit with the local building department.
  5. Get the material and workmanship warranties in writing.
