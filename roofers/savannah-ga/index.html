Roofing in Savannah: what to know

Savannah is a coastal city exposed to hurricanes, tropical storms and salt air. Its historic district has many older homes with metal and slate roofs, which need roofers experienced with those materials and historic review rules.

More broadly, Savannah is in the hurricane and high-wind coast. The most common causes of roof damage in this kind of climate are:

  • Hurricane and tropical-storm winds that lift and tear shingles.
  • Wind-driven rain that gets under flashing.
  • Heat and humidity that age shingles and underlayment faster.

Popular roof types around Savannah

Many homeowners here choose high-wind-rated architectural shingles, concrete or clay tile, or standing-seam metal. Ask roofers about wind ratings, secondary water barriers and how the roof deck is fastened.

Local tip: After a named storm, report damage to your insurer quickly and be careful with out-of-town crews going door to door. Some insurers here ask about a roof's age before renewing a policy, so keep your installation paperwork.

Checking a roofer's license in Georgia

Georgia doesn't have a roofing-specific state license. Larger residential jobs generally fall under the state's residential and general contractor licensing board, and many counties and cities require a business license and permit, so ask which licenses the roofer holds.

Before you hire a roofer in Savannah

  1. Verify the license or registration as described above, and make sure the name matches the contract.
  2. Ask for current certificates of liability and workers' compensation insurance.
  3. Get a written, itemized quote and compare at least two.
  4. Make sure the roofer pulls the permit with the local building department.
  5. Get the material and workmanship warranties in writing.
