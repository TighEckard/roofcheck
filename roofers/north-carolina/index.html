Roofing in North Carolina

Much of North Carolina falls in the hot, humid Southeast. Common roof problems include:

  • Summer thunderstorms and the occasional tropical system.
  • Humidity that feeds algae streaks and attic moisture.
  • Heat that shortens the life of shingles.

Algae-resistant architectural shingles are the most common choice, with metal roofs growing in popularity. Proper attic ventilation helps shingles last longer in the heat.

Tip: Black streaks are usually algae, not damage, but curling, missing or granule-shedding shingles are worth an inspection.

Checking a roofer's license in North Carolina

Licensing rules for roofers differ by state and sometimes by city or county. Ask every contractor for their license number and look it up with the North Carolina licensing authority or your local building department before you sign. Our roofer checklist covers the rest.
