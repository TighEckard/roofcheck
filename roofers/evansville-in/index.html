What wears out roofs around Evansville

  • Winter ice storms and freeze-thaw cycles that crack shingles and bring down limbs.
  • Severe spring storms with straight-line winds, hail and the occasional tornado.
  • Summer thunderstorms and humidity that speed up shingle aging and algae streaks.

Best roofing materials for Evansville

Around Evansville, roofers often recommend wind-rated architectural shingles installed with the manufacturer's full nailing pattern, and algae-resistant architectural shingles and good attic ventilation.

Best time to replace a roof in Evansville

Severe storm season peaks in spring, roughly March through June, so late winter is a good time to have loose or worn shingles fixed. After a big storm, check for missing shingles and dented vents and gutters.

Local tip: Black streaks are usually algae, not damage, but curling, missing or granule-shedding shingles are worth an inspection.

Checking a roofer's license in Indiana

Indiana doesn't license roofers statewide. Some cities and counties require local contractor licensing or registration, so ask what the roofer holds where you live. Our roofer checklist covers the rest.

Evansville roofing questions

How much does a new roof cost in Evansville?
Nationally, Angi data reported by NerdWallet puts the average near $9,500, with most jobs between about $5,800 and $46,000. Your price in Evansville depends on size, pitch, material and local labor, so get a written quote.
Will insurance pay for roof damage in Evansville?
Most homeowners policies cover sudden hail and wind damage, but in storm-prone areas like Evansville many policies carry a separate wind and hail deductible, and some pay only depreciated value on older roofs.
Do I need a permit to replace my roof in Evansville?
Usually, yes. Your roofer should pull it with the Evansville building department or Vanderburgh County and show it to you before work starts.
