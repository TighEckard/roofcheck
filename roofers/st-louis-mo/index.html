Roofing in St. Louis: what to know

St. Louis sits in the hail and severe-storm belt, and that shapes what wears roofs out here and what homeowners tend to install. The most common causes of roof damage in this kind of climate are:

  • Hail that bruises and cracks shingles, sometimes without an obvious leak.
  • Spring and summer thunderstorms with strong straight-line winds.
  • Big temperature swings that stress roofing materials.

Popular roof types around St. Louis

Impact-resistant (often called Class 4) shingles and metal roofing are popular here. Some insurers offer premium discounts for impact-rated roofs, so ask your agent.

Local tip: Hail damage isn't always visible from the ground. If a storm hit your neighborhood, a roofer's inspection with photos can show what's there before you call your insurer.

Before you hire a roofer in St. Louis

  1. Verify the contractor's license with the Missouri or local licensing authority. Rules differ by state and city.
  2. Ask for current certificates of liability and workers' compensation insurance.
  3. Get a written, itemized quote and compare at least two.
  4. Make sure the roofer pulls the permit with the local building department.
  5. Get the material and workmanship warranties in writing.

St. Louis roofing questions

How do I find a good roofer in St. Louis?
Get two or three written quotes, check each contractor's license with the Missouri or local licensing authority, ask for proof of liability and workers' comp insurance, and read the warranty terms. Our roofer checklist walks you through it.
How much does a new roof cost in St. Louis?
It depends on the size, pitch and material of your roof and on local labor costs. Nationally, Angi data reported by NerdWallet puts the average around $9,500, with most jobs between about $5,800 and $46,000. A written quote after an inspection is the only way to know your price in St. Louis.
Is the roof inspection free?
Yes. Requests through Bedrock Roof are for a free inspection and quote, with no obligation to hire.
Do I need a permit to replace my roof in St. Louis?
Most U.S. cities require a building permit for a full roof replacement. Ask your roofer to pull the permit with the St. Louis or county building department and show it to you.
