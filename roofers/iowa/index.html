Common roof problems in Iowa

  • Hailstorms that bruise and crack shingles, sometimes without an obvious leak.
  • Snow loads and ice dams that push meltwater under shingles at the eaves.

Impact-resistant (often called Class 4) shingles and metal roofing are popular here. Some insurers offer premium discounts for impact-rated roofs, so ask your agent.

Tip: Hail damage isn't always visible from the ground. If a storm hit your neighborhood, a roofer's inspection with photos can show what's there before you call your insurer.

Checking a roofer's license in Iowa

Iowa requires contractors, including roofers, to register with the state Division of Labor. You can search contractor registrations online.

Ask every contractor for their license or registration number and check it before you sign. Our roofer checklist covers the rest.
