Roofing in Sacramento: what to know

Sacramento has hot, dry summers and wet winters, and the region is surrounded by wildfire-prone areas. Fire-rated roofing and ember-resistant vents are worth asking about, along with good attic ventilation for the summer heat.

What wears out roofs around Sacramento

  • Intense sun and heat that dry out and crack shingles and underlayment.
  • Wildfire embers in dry seasons that can ignite roofs and gutters.

Best roofing materials for Sacramento

Around Sacramento, roofers often recommend tile, light-colored or reflective 'cool roof' materials, and coatings on flat sections, and Class A fire-rated roofing and ember-resistant vents.

Best time to replace a roof in Sacramento

Summer heat makes roof work slow and hard on materials, so many homeowners here schedule replacements for fall, winter or spring.

Local tip: Ask whether a tile roof needs a full replacement or an underlayment 'lift and relay', which reuses good tiles.

Checking a roofer's license in California

California roofers must be licensed by the Contractors State License Board (CSLB), usually with a C-39 roofing classification. The CSLB website shows license status, bonds, workers' comp and any complaints. Our roofer checklist covers the rest.

Sacramento roofing questions

How much does a new roof cost in Sacramento?
Nationally, Angi data reported by NerdWallet puts the average near $9,500, with most jobs between about $5,800 and $46,000. Your price in Sacramento depends on size, pitch, material and local labor, so get a written quote.
Will insurance pay for roof damage in Sacramento?
Most homeowners policies cover sudden damage, such as a tree falling on the roof or a storm tearing off shingles, but not wear and tear or an old roof failing.
Do I need a permit to replace my roof in Sacramento?
Usually, yes. Your roofer should pull it with the Sacramento building department and show it to you before work starts.
