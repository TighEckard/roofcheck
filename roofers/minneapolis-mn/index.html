What wears out roofs around Minneapolis

  • Hailstorms that bruise and crack shingles, sometimes without an obvious leak.
  • Snow loads and ice dams that push meltwater under shingles at the eaves.

Best roofing materials for Minneapolis

Around Minneapolis, roofers often recommend impact-resistant (often Class 4) shingles or metal, which some insurers discount, and an ice-and-water barrier along the eaves plus good attic insulation and ventilation.

Best time to replace a roof in Minneapolis

Hail season peaks in spring and early summer. Have the roof checked after any storm with hail, since bruised shingles often don't leak until months later, and insurance claims usually have filing deadlines.

Local tip: Hail damage isn't always visible from the ground. If a storm hit your neighborhood, a roofer's inspection with photos can show what's there before you call your insurer.

Checking a roofer's license in Minnesota

Minnesota requires residential roofers to be licensed by the Department of Labor and Industry (DLI). You can look up a license on the DLI website. Our roofer checklist covers the rest.

Minneapolis roofing questions

How much does a new roof cost in Minneapolis?
Nationally, Angi data reported by NerdWallet puts the average near $9,500, with most jobs between about $5,800 and $46,000. Your price in Minneapolis depends on size, pitch, material and local labor, so get a written quote.
Will insurance pay for roof damage in Minneapolis?
Most homeowners policies cover sudden hail and wind damage, but in storm-prone areas like Minneapolis many policies carry a separate wind and hail deductible, and some pay only depreciated value on older roofs.
Do I need a permit to replace my roof in Minneapolis?
Usually, yes. Your roofer should pull it with the Minneapolis building department or Hennepin County and show it to you before work starts.
