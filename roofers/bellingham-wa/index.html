Roofing in Bellingham: what to know

Bellingham, near the Canadian border on Bellingham Bay, has long, wet winters and occasional strong outflow winds from the Fraser Valley. Moss, moisture and wind are the main roof concerns.

What wears out roofs around Bellingham

  • Salt air near the water that corrodes metal flashing, fasteners and vents.
  • Long wet seasons, moss and algae that hold moisture against the roof.

Best roofing materials for Bellingham

Around Bellingham, roofers often recommend corrosion-resistant flashing and fasteners, and moss-resistant shingles or metal, with careful flashing around chimneys and skylights.

Best time to replace a roof in Bellingham

The rainy season runs roughly October through May, so summer is the easiest time for a replacement. Book early, since dry-season schedules fill up.

Local tip: Dry summer months are the busiest season for roofers, so book inspections early.

Checking a roofer's license in Washington

Washington requires contractors, including roofers, to register with the Department of Labor & Industries (L&I), which also shows bond and insurance status. Look the roofer up on the L&I website. Our roofer checklist covers the rest.

Bellingham roofing questions

How much does a new roof cost in Bellingham?
Nationally, Angi data reported by NerdWallet puts the average near $9,500, with most jobs between about $5,800 and $46,000. Your price in Bellingham depends on size, pitch, material and local labor, so get a written quote.
Will insurance pay for roof damage in Bellingham?
Most homeowners policies cover sudden damage, such as a tree falling on the roof or a storm tearing off shingles, but not wear and tear or an old roof failing.
Do I need a permit to replace my roof in Bellingham?
Usually, yes. Your roofer should pull it with the Bellingham building department or Whatcom County and show it to you before work starts.
