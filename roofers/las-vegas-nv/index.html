Roofing in Las Vegas: what to know

Las Vegas has extreme summer heat and intense UV, which break down roofing materials quickly. Concrete tile and foam or coated flat roofs are common, and monsoon storms in late summer can bring sudden wind and heavy rain.

Best roofing materials for Las Vegas

Around Las Vegas, roofers often recommend tile, light-colored or reflective 'cool roof' materials, and coatings on flat sections.

Best time to replace a roof in Las Vegas

Summer heat makes roof work slow and hard on materials, so many homeowners here schedule replacements for fall, winter or spring.

Local tip: Ask whether a tile roof needs a full replacement or an underlayment 'lift and relay', which reuses good tiles.

Las Vegas roofing questions

How much does a new roof cost in Las Vegas?
Nationally, Angi data reported by NerdWallet puts the average near $9,500, with most jobs between about $5,800 and $46,000. Your price in Las Vegas depends on size, pitch, material and local labor, so get a written quote.
Will insurance pay for roof damage in Las Vegas?
Most homeowners policies cover sudden damage, such as a tree falling on the roof or a storm tearing off shingles, but not wear and tear or an old roof failing.
Do I need a permit to replace my roof in Las Vegas?
Usually, yes. Your roofer should pull it with the Las Vegas building department or Clark County and show it to you before work starts.
