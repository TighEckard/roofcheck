Roofing in Arlington: what to know

Arlington sits across the Potomac from Washington, D.C., with many brick colonials, rowhouses and older homes from the 1930s to 1950s. Nor'easters, summer thunderstorms and the occasional winter ice storm are the main roof stresses, and slate and older shingle roofs here are often due for repair or replacement.

What wears out roofs around Arlington

  • Hurricane and tropical-storm winds that lift shingles and drive rain under flashing.
  • Snow loads and ice dams that push meltwater under shingles at the eaves.
  • Summer thunderstorms and humidity that speed up shingle aging and algae streaks.

Best roofing materials for Arlington

Around Arlington, roofers often recommend high-wind-rated shingles, tile or standing-seam metal with a sealed roof deck, an ice-and-water barrier along the eaves plus good attic insulation and ventilation, and algae-resistant architectural shingles and good attic ventilation.

Best time to replace a roof in Arlington

Atlantic hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30, so the best time to fix weak spots is late winter or spring, before it starts. After a named storm, roofers get booked up fast.

Local tip: After a named storm, report damage to your insurer quickly and be careful with out-of-town crews going door to door. Some insurers here ask about a roof's age before renewing a policy, so keep your installation paperwork.

Checking a roofer's license in Virginia

Virginia requires contractors, including roofers, to be licensed by the Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation (DPOR). You can verify the license and its class online. Our roofer checklist covers the rest.

Arlington roofing questions

How much does a new roof cost in Arlington?
Nationally, Angi data reported by NerdWallet puts the average near $9,500, with most jobs between about $5,800 and $46,000. Your price in Arlington depends on size, pitch, material and local labor, so get a written quote.
Will insurance pay for roof damage in Arlington?
Most homeowners policies cover sudden storm damage, but many policies in hurricane-prone areas like Arlington carry a separate hurricane or named-storm deductible, often a percentage of the home's insured value rather than a flat amount.
Do I need a permit to replace my roof in Arlington?
Usually, yes. Your roofer should pull it with the Arlington building department or Arlington County and show it to you before work starts.
