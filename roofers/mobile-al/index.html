Roofing in Mobile: what to know

Mobile is the third-largest city in Alabama, with roughly 194,000 residents. It has a humid subtropical climate, with hot, humid summers and cooler winters. It sits right by the Gulf of Mexico, so salt air and coastal storms are part of the picture for roofs here.

What wears out roofs around Mobile

  • Hurricane and tropical-storm winds that lift shingles and drive rain under flashing.
  • Salt air near the water that corrodes metal flashing, fasteners and vents.

Popular roof choices around Mobile

Around Mobile, roofers often recommend high-wind-rated shingles, tile or standing-seam metal with a sealed roof deck, and corrosion-resistant flashing and fasteners.

Local tip: After a named storm, report damage to your insurer quickly and be careful with out-of-town crews going door to door. Some insurers here ask about a roof's age before renewing a policy, so keep your installation paperwork.

Checking a roofer's license in Alabama

Alabama's Home Builders Licensure Board licenses residential roofers on larger jobs, and roofers must also follow local business license rules. Ask for the license number and check it on the board's website.

Before you hire a roofer in Mobile

  1. Verify the license or registration as described above, and make sure the name matches the contract.
  2. Ask for current certificates of liability and workers' compensation insurance.
  3. Get a written, itemized quote and compare at least two.
  4. Make sure the roofer pulls the permit with the local building department.
  5. Get the material and workmanship warranties in writing.
