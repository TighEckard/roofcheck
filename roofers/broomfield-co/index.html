Best roofing materials for Broomfield

Around Broomfield, roofers often recommend impact-resistant (often Class 4) shingles or metal, which some insurers discount, an ice-and-water barrier along the eaves plus good attic insulation and ventilation, and Class A fire-rated roofing and ember-resistant vents.

Best time to replace a roof in Broomfield

Hail season peaks in spring and early summer. Have the roof checked after any storm with hail, since bruised shingles often don't leak until months later, and insurance claims usually have filing deadlines.

Local tip: Hail damage isn't always visible from the ground. If a storm hit your neighborhood, a roofer's inspection with photos can show what's there before you call your insurer.

Checking a roofer's license in Colorado

Colorado has no statewide roofing license, but many cities, including Denver and Colorado Springs, license or register roofing contractors locally. A good inspection covers the shingles or membrane, flashing, vents, gutters and the attic.
