Roofing in El Monte: what to know

El Monte is one of California's larger cities, with roughly 117,000 residents, about 12 miles from Los Angeles. It has a semi-arid climate, with dry air, strong sun and limited rain. It's about 25 miles from the Pacific Ocean.

What wears out roofs around El Monte

  • Intense sun and heat that dry out and crack shingles and underlayment.
  • Wildfire embers in dry seasons that can ignite roofs and gutters.

Popular roof choices around El Monte

Around El Monte, roofers often recommend tile, light-colored or reflective 'cool roof' materials, and coatings on flat sections, and Class A fire-rated roofing and ember-resistant vents.

Local tip: Ask whether a tile roof needs a full replacement or an underlayment 'lift and relay', which reuses good tiles.

Checking a roofer's license in California

California roofers must be licensed by the Contractors State License Board (CSLB), usually with a C-39 roofing classification. The CSLB website shows license status, bonds, workers' comp and any complaints.

Before you hire a roofer in El Monte

  1. Verify the license or registration as described above, and make sure the name matches the contract.
  2. Ask for current certificates of liability and workers' compensation insurance.
  3. Get a written, itemized quote and compare at least two.
  4. Make sure the roofer pulls the permit with the local building department.
  5. Get the material and workmanship warranties in writing.
