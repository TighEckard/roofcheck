TPO vs. EPDM vs. PVC: which flat roof membrane is best?

The three most common single-ply membranes on U.S. commercial roofs, side by side.

Updated November 18, 2026 · By the Bedrock Roof team

Quick answer: TPO is the most common choice on new low-slope commercial roofs. It's usually white and reflective, with heat-welded seams. EPDM is a rubber membrane with the longest track record, often used in colder climates and on buildings where cooling cost matters less. PVC costs more but resists grease and chemicals, so it's a common pick for restaurants and some industrial roofs. Installation quality, especially at seams, edges and penetrations, matters as much as which membrane you choose.

How to choose

  • Climate and energy: a white membrane reflects sunlight and can lower cooling loads in hot regions. Some local energy codes require a reflective ("cool") roof on low-slope commercial buildings.
  • Roof traffic: if HVAC techs are on the roof often, ask about thicker membranes and walkway pads.
  • Exhaust and grease: kitchen exhaust can damage EPDM and TPO; PVC or a protective pad near fans is common.
  • Attachment: membranes can be mechanically fastened, fully adhered or ballasted. Wind exposure, deck type and budget decide which.
  • Insulation: a replacement is often the time to add insulation under the membrane to meet current energy code.

Questions for bidders

  • What membrane thickness (mils) and attachment method are you quoting?
  • Is this a manufacturer-warranted system, and for how many years?
  • Will you remove the existing roof or recover over it, and did you take core samples to check for wet insulation?

Frequently asked questions

Is TPO better than EPDM?
Neither is best everywhere. TPO is reflective with welded seams and suits hot climates. EPDM has a long track record and suits colder climates and tighter budgets.
Which flat roof membrane lasts longest?
Lifespans overlap and depend heavily on installation, thickness and maintenance. Ask each bidder what warranty term the manufacturer will issue for the exact system quoted.
Why do restaurants use PVC roofs?
PVC resists grease and many chemicals better than TPO or EPDM, which matters near kitchen exhaust fans.
Can a new membrane go over the old roof?
Sometimes. Codes usually limit the number of roof layers, and wet insulation must be removed first. Core samples or a moisture survey tell you what's under there.
