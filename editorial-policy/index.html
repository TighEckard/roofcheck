Editorial policy

How we write and check the guides and city pages on Bedrock Roof.

Who writes our content

Bedrock Roof's guides and city pages are written and edited by the Bedrock Roof team. We are a referral service, not a roofing contractor, so our guides explain how roofing, hiring and insurance usually work. They are not a substitute for an in-person inspection or for professional insurance or legal advice.

How we research

  • Costs come from published sources, which we name and link, such as Angi data reported by NerdWallet. We don't invent prices or averages.
  • Licensing rules come from state licensing agencies. Rules change, so every page tells you to check the license with your state or city before hiring.
  • City facts: population comes from GeoNames, climate type from the Köppen-Geiger classification, and county from U.S. Census boundaries. Local notes on storms and conditions are written by hand from public records and news coverage of major events.
  • Insurance: we explain common policy terms. Your policy and insurer decide what's covered.

What we won't publish

  • Made-up reviews, testimonials, project photos or job counts.
  • Claims that any roofer is licensed, insured or "certified" unless the homeowner can verify it.
  • Promises about insurance approval.

How we make money

Homeowners never pay us. Roofing contractors may pay us for introductions to homeowners who asked to be contacted. That doesn't change what our guides say. We recommend getting more than one quote and checking every contractor, including any we introduce you to.

Corrections

If something on this site is wrong or out of date, email hello@bedrockroof.com. We review every report and update the page and its date when we make a change.
