What happens during a roof inspection?

Know what a good inspection covers so you can tell a thorough roofer from a rushed sales pitch.

Updated November 18, 2026 · By the Bedrock Roof team

Quick answer: A roof inspection usually takes 30 to 90 minutes for a typical house. A roofer checks the shingles or panels, flashing, vents, gutters, attic and signs of leaks, takes photos, and then tells you whether the roof needs a repair, a replacement or nothing yet. Ask for the photos and a written summary either way.

What gets checked

  • Roof covering: missing, cracked, curling or bruised shingles; granule loss; loose or dented metal panels.
  • Flashing: around chimneys, skylights, walls and valleys, where most leaks start.
  • Penetrations: pipe boots, vents and exhaust fans.
  • Gutters and drainage: clogs, sagging and granules collecting in gutters.
  • Attic (if accessible): daylight through boards, water stains, mold, ventilation and insulation.
  • Decking: soft or spongy spots that suggest rot.

What you should get afterward

  1. Photos of any damage, ideally with something for scale.
  2. A plain explanation: repair, replace, or monitor.
  3. A written quote that lists materials, scope, permit, cleanup and warranty if work is recommended.

Questions to ask

  • Can you show me the damage in the photos?
  • Is this storm damage, age, or an installation problem?
  • What happens if I wait a year?
  • Is a repair realistic, or would you replace?

Red flags

Be careful if someone damages the roof during the inspection, won't show you photos, or pushes you to sign a contract or insurance paperwork before you've had time to think. See roofing scams.

When to get one

After a major hail or wind storm, before buying a home, when your roof is 15+ years old, or any time you see a leak or stain. Commercial roofs should be checked twice a year; see our commercial checklist.

Frequently asked questions

Is a roof inspection free?
Many roofers offer free inspections when you're considering a repair or replacement. A home-sale inspection or a certified report for insurance may cost a fee.
How long does a roof inspection take?
Often 30 to 90 minutes for a typical house. Larger or steeper roofs and attic checks take longer.
Do I need to be home?
It helps, so the roofer can check the attic and show you photos, but many will inspect the outside without you there.
Can a drone inspect my roof?
Yes, some roofers use drones for steep or fragile roofs. A drone can't check the attic or feel for soft decking, so it's not always a full substitute.
