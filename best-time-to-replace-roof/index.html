When is the best time of year to replace a roof?

Weather, demand and your region all affect when a roof replacement goes smoothest.

Updated November 18, 2026 · By the Bedrock Roof team

Quick answer: Late spring through early fall is the most common roofing season in most of the U.S., with fall often the sweet spot: mild temperatures help shingles seal, and storms are less frequent in many areas. Roofers can install year-round in warm southern climates. If your roof is leaking or storm-damaged, don't wait for the "best" season; a temporary fix or replacement now usually costs less than water damage later.

By season

  • Spring: good weather in many places, but rain can cause delays and spring hail can create a backlog.
  • Summer: long days and dry weather in much of the country; very high heat can make shingles easier to scuff and is hard on crews.
  • Fall: often ideal. Moderate temperatures help self-sealing shingle strips bond before winter.
  • Winter: possible in mild climates. In cold regions, crews may limit work, and shingles may need hand-sealing.

By region

  • Gulf and Atlantic coasts: try to finish before peak hurricane season (August to October).
  • Hail belt (Texas to the Dakotas, Colorado): demand spikes after spring and summer hail; booking early helps.
  • Northern states: fall is popular because of winter; book early.
  • Desert Southwest: spring and fall avoid the hottest months.

Scheduling tips

  1. Get quotes a month or two before you want the work.
  2. Ask about the roofer's backlog and weather-delay policy.
  3. Don't sign with a door-knocker just because a storm just passed. See roofing scams.

Frequently asked questions

Can a roof be replaced in winter?
Yes in many places. Roofers watch the temperature and may hand-seal shingles in cold weather. In snowy regions, winter jobs are less common.
Is it cheaper to replace a roof in the off-season?
Sometimes. Some roofers have more scheduling flexibility in slower months, but material prices don't drop much by season.
How far ahead should I book a roofer?
Several weeks is common, and longer after a big regional storm. Getting quotes early gives you time to compare.
Should I wait to replace a leaking roof?
No. Get the leak stopped now, even with a temporary repair, to prevent damage inside the house.
