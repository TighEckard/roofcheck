Privacy policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

This policy explains what information Bedrock Roof ("we") collects on bedrockroof.com and how we use it.

Information you give us

When you request a quote we collect your name, mobile phone number, ZIP code, the type of help you need and any note you add.

Information collected automatically

We use our own cookieless analytics to understand how the site is used: pages viewed, how you arrived (for example a search engine or a link), device and browser type, approximate city and region based on your IP address, scroll depth and clicks, and whether a form was started or sent. We don't store your IP address with these records. We honor Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals by turning this analytics off. The site uses a third-party service (ipwho.is) to estimate your city from your IP address.

How we use and share it

  • To pass your request to an independent roofing contractor serving your area so they can contact you about your roof. Contractors may pay us for these introductions.
  • To contact you about your request by call or text, with your consent.
  • To run, secure and improve the site.
  • When required by law.

We don't sell your information to data brokers or marketing lists. Once a contractor receives your request, their use of it is governed by their own privacy practices.

Calls and texts

You can opt out of texts by replying STOP, and you can ask us or the contractor to stop calling at any time.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email hello@bedrockroof.com. Depending on your state, you may have additional rights.

Data storage and security

Requests are stored with a cloud database provider and kept only as long as needed for the purposes above. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

This site is for adults and isn't directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

BedrockRoof

A free referral service that connects homeowners across the U.S. with independent local roofing contractors.

Bedrock Roof is not a roofing contractor and does not perform, guarantee or warranty any roofing work. Contractors are independent businesses and may pay us for introductions. Always check a contractor's license, insurance and references before you sign anything.
